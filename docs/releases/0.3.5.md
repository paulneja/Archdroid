# ArchDroid v0.3.5

- Richer press feedback, touch lighting and staggered card entrances across the app.
- Persistent headings in Desktop and Settings, with a reading-position line on long pages.
- Animated radio and checkbox selections in Desktop, language, init and protection settings.
